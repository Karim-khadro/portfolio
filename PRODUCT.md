# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The primary audience is the non-technical owner of a one-to-fifteen-person business, usually in the Liège area, who wants a website, web application, or useful AI integration without having to manage several suppliers. Recruiters have a separate, clearly secondary path at `/cv`.

## Product Purpose

Karim Khadro's site explains his offer in plain language, makes AI work concrete, and gives a visitor enough confidence to book a call or send a qualified project request. A local business owner should understand what Karim does, how he works, and what an engagement costs within thirty seconds.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, precise, and accountable. The voice is warm and direct, with the confidence of one experienced person taking responsibility from first conversation to delivery. It avoids agency theatre and technical posturing.

## Anti-references

- Generic developer portfolios that lead with a technology stack or skill ratings.
- Generic SaaS landing pages made from repeated icon cards, gradients, and empty superlatives.
- AI marketing built on hype, vague automation claims, or invented proof.
- Agency-style distance that obscures who will actually do the work.
- Hidden pricing, inflated promises, and dark patterns around booking or contact.

## Design Principles

- Lead with one clear promise, then reveal detail only when it helps a decision.
- Show how the work moves from a real business problem to a useful result.
- Use plain language and concrete examples before technical vocabulary.
- Make personal accountability visible throughout the site.
- Earn trust through honest scope, pricing, privacy, and evidence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve complete keyboard navigation, visible focus states, semantic structure, sufficient contrast, bilingual content parity, and a reduced-motion experience that communicates the same story without animation. Never rely on color alone to communicate state.
